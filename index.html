Privacy Policy

Saturn Games. Last updated: November 11, 2026

This policy explains what information is collected when you play mobile games published by Saturn Games (the "Games"), including Cannons And Pirates, Gorilla Smash, Cyber Samurai, Superhero Simulator and Dragon Rider, and how it is used.

Information we collect

We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or any account. Game progress (levels, gold, unlocked items and settings) is stored only on your device.

Advertising

The Games are free and show ads. Ads are delivered by Google AdMob and, in some Games, through the CAS.AI mediation platform (Clever Ads Solutions) and the ad networks it works with, such as AppLovin, Unity Ads, Mintegral, Pangle, IronSource and Meta Audience Network. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, these partners may collect:

  • Device information: device model, operating system version, language, screen size
  • The advertising ID of your device (Google Advertising ID)
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Information about ad interactions: ads shown, clicks, video completions
  • Diagnostic data such as crash and performance information of the ad SDKs

Each partner processes this data under its own privacy policy. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services, Google's advertising policies and, for CAS.AI and its partners, cas.ai/privacy-policy.

Your choices and consent

  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the Games ask for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time with the PRIVACY button in the game settings.
  • You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads).
  • Residents of California and other US states can opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for advertising using the same device settings.

Children

The Games are not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a Game, contact us and we will help remove it.

Data retention and deletion

We do not keep personal data on our own servers. Uninstalling a Game removes all game data from your device. To request deletion of data held by an advertising partner, use the partner's own privacy controls or contact us and we will forward your request.

Security

Data sent by the ad SDKs is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
